About & Concept

A Tour Company With Heart

Big-platform tours show you the postcard view. We show you the Kobe we actually grew up in — the back-street sake breweries, the harbor light at dusk, the shopkeepers who know our names.

Small groups, real conversation

We cap group sizes so you can actually talk to your guide — and to each other.

Local routes, not tourist loops

Food, craft & culture, not just sights

From Nada sake breweries to family-run kitchens — we go where the culture actually lives.
